01. About me

I'm a graduate student in Computer Science & Engineering at Penn State and a Graduate Teaching Assistant for CMPSC 360 (Discrete Mathematics), after a semester as a Learning Assistant for CMPSC 200 (Programming for Engineers with MATLAB). My work spans deep learning for audio-visual understanding, and the less glamorous-but-essential engineering that makes systems reliable in production.

Before grad school I spent two and a half years at IDFC First Bank, growing from intern to full-time engineer while building payment APIs in Go, anomaly-detection tooling, and analytics integrations that drove measurable revenue. Most recently, I interned as an AI Engineer at Canyontechs AI, working on a production log-monitoring agent.

I like problems where careful modeling meets careful systems design — when a clever cascade saves a GPU forward pass, or a well-placed acknowledgement keeps a message from being lost.
